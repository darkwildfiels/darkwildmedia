## FAQ
**Will I receive a physical item?** No. This is a digital download – nothing will be shipped.
**How do I get my files?** Right after payment, Etsy gives you a download link (Purchases & Reviews page and your email).
**What paper size?** US Letter (8.5x11 in) and A4 are included. Print at 100% / "fit to page".
**Can I print more than once?** Yes, as many times as you like for personal use.
**Can I sell the printed pages?** No – files are for personal, non-commercial use only. Classroom use is fine.
**Custom pet/family pages: what photo should I send?** A clear, well-lit photo where the face is visible. We send a preview within 24–48 hours and make one round of edits for free.
**Refunds?** Because files are downloaded instantly, we can't accept returns. If anything is wrong with a file, message us and we'll fix it right away.
